Bedrock notes app v0.1.3: sync is off until you turn it on, and Toolbay can't read your synced notes.

**Changed**
- Sync no longer turns itself on when you're signed in to Toolbay. It stays off until you turn it on in Settings › Sync. With sync off, nothing from your vault is uploaded.
- Turning sync on asks you to choose a sync passphrase. Your notes, file names and folder names are encrypted on your computer with a key only that passphrase opens, so Toolbay stores a locked copy it can't read. You type the passphrase once on each of your computers.
- If you forget the passphrase, your synced notes can't be recovered from Toolbay, by you or by us. The files on your computers are never affected: "Forgot your passphrase?" starts the synced copy over with a new passphrase, and your notes upload again.

**If you used sync before this update**
- Earlier versions stored your vault's key in your Toolbay account without a passphrase, so Toolbay could have unlocked your synced notes. This update deletes that key from your account (toolbay.ai also refuses to store it again).
- Sync is off after the update, and Bedrock asks you once whether to turn it back on. Nothing uploads until you choose.
- When you turn it back on, your synced notes get a brand-new key: Bedrock downloads them with the old key, erases the old copy (including its version history and deleted files), and uploads everything again locked with your passphrase. The old key can't open anything any more.
- Older versions of Bedrock stop syncing until they're updated; your notes on those computers stay as they are.

**Mac:** Bedrock no longer uses the macOS Keychain, so it stops asking for your login password after updates. It reads your Toolbay sign-in from the file the Toolbay app (0.1.13 or later) keeps, and keeps its own sync key in a file only your user account can read.

**Unchanged:** nothing runs in the background. Closing the window quits Bedrock, and sync and agent access stop when it quits.

**Parity with Obsidian:** 620 of 669 items in docs/FEATURES.md are done, including 5 marked not applicable (Publish and the mobile app). Unchanged from v0.1.2.
