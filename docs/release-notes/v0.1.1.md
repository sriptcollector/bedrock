Bedrock notes app v0.1.1: a setup guide that turns your vault into a personal wiki written by your Claude.

**New**
- A short setup guide on first start (reopen it any time with the command "Open onboarding" or Settings › General › Setup guide):
  1. A prompt to give your Claude. It reads what it knows about you (its memory, GitHub, your project folders, connected email and calendar) and writes a linked wiki in your vault: Home, About me, Projects, People, Companies, Skills and a Timeline, each note colored by life area with an icon. Secrets are never copied into notes.
  2. An optional prompt that has Claude set up a daily update: it logs what you did that day in a daily note and keeps your project and people notes current.
  3. Make it yours: graph colors (Ocean, Sunset, Forest, Neon, Mono, Candy), app colors with light or dark mode, and a theme (Pixelated, Minimal, Animals, Halloween, or let Claude design one for you). Changes show right away and sync like your other settings.
- `Bedrock/Instructions for Claude.md`: Bedrock leaves requests and your preferences there, and Claude checks them off the next time it runs.
- Graph view: a note's `icon` property shows above its node, and its `color` property colors the node when no color group matches. Graph colors also update live when graph.json changes on disk.
